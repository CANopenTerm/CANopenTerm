Generative AI, including large language models (LLMs), may be used as a tool
when working on CANopenTerm, but all contributed code must be written by the
contributor and must not be AI-generated.

We value genuine human contributions and want to engage with people who
understand and take responsibility for the code they submit. AI tools may be
used for assistance, learning, research, or other supporting tasks, as long as
the resulting contributions, including code and comments, are authored by the
contributor. Please do not submit AI-generated code or comments in bug reports
or pull requests.

Any pull request to CANopenTerm will require you to confirm that you are the
author of your contribution and that you submit it under the MIT License.
